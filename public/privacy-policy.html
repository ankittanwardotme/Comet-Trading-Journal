Privacy Policy

Comet Trading Journal · Last updated September 18, 2026

Comet Trading Journal ("the app") is a personal trading journal application. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture (via the default openid email profile OAuth scopes only — we do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data).
  • Trading data you enter: trades, checklist entries, notes, reminders, fund transactions, and any chart screenshots or images you choose to upload.

How your information is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's functionality to you — storing and displaying your own trading journal. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or trading data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase-hosted Postgres database and Supabase Storage, protected by row-level security so that each user can only access their own data. Images you upload are stored in private storage buckets scoped to your account.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. The app provides in-app controls to delete individual trades, notes, reminders, and other records. If you would like your entire account and associated data permanently deleted, contact us using the email below.

Third-party services

The app uses Google (for sign-in) and Supabase (for hosting, database, and storage). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices, and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Not financial advice

This app is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide financial, investment, or tax advice, and nothing in the app should be interpreted as such.
